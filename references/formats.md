# Format guidance

Read the relevant section. Dimensions here are arithmetic examples, not current platform upload requirements. Verify destination limits when they affect acceptance.

## Thumbnails
Pair title and image around one truthful promise. Let the image demonstrate rather than merely repeat the title. Portraits are optional unless required; expression and gaze should serve the message. Remove UI fragments and badges that become noise. Inspect a realistic small preview, for example 320 pixels wide, and expected overlays when known. Imagined screenshots cannot serve as product proof.

## Posts and carousels
A post needs one main idea. Map every carousel page to a job before illustration: opening promise, context, development, evidence/example, payoff, and optional action. Adapt to the requested count; this is not a fixed sequence. Establish type roles, spacing, palette functions, image treatment, and navigation cues. Vary composition to fit content while retaining continuity. Export ordered individual pages and inspect each; a contact sheet alone cannot validate small text.

## Campaigns, products, covers, and characters
Create a master system, then recompose for actual crops rather than blindly resizing. Preserve product geometry, labels, approved marks, and defining character cues. Record allowed character variation. A recurring mascot is not compulsory for unrelated work. Distinguish illustration from documentary evidence.

## Typography and Urdu/RTL
Follow the requested language. Roman Urdu uses left-to-right layout; Urdu script requires right-to-left shaping and a suitable font. Do not substitute Arabic wording for Urdu. Inspect joined letters, punctuation, diacritics, mixed English/numerals, line breaks, and reading order in the actual pixels. Do not reverse strings or mirror logos to imitate RTL. Check glyph coverage first. If reliable shaping is unavailable, disclose it and supply exact copy separately rather than claiming correct final lettering.

Choose type for information roles and character. Test contrast over the actual background. When accessibility compliance is requested, verify the applicable requirements and measure them rather than claiming compliance by appearance. Supply alt text when requested or relevant to accessibility delivery.

## Print and exports
For print, resolve finished size, bleed, margins, effective resolution, color profile, and font handling against printer requirements. Changing an extension does not make RGB artwork print-ready. Distinguish raster, vector, and editable outputs. Verify actual alpha transparency rather than a drawn checkerboard. Check dimensions independently of captions: 1080 x 1350 is 4:5; 1080 x 1440 is 3:4. Use explicit page ordering and versioned names.
